Uncertainty-aware Bayesian fusion and hierarchical voting for robust audio-visual speech recognition.

arXiv ↗

UBG-Net models signal-level and model uncertainty for audio-visual fusion. At inference, hierarchical voting selects a transcript from Monte Carlo samples. The framework is evaluated on AVCocktail and LRS2.
